Privacy policy

Skribly is designed as a local-first Windows application. This policy describes what the current beta stores, what the website processes, and what will change before paid checkout is enabled.

1. Application data

The current Windows beta stores note text, note metadata, positions and context information on your computer. Attachments and drawings are also stored locally in the application’s WebView data profile. Normal note use does not require a Skribly account or cloud service.

2. Context information

To attach notes to applications, Skribly reads limited information about open windows, such as the process name, window title, window class, position, size and temporary native window handle. This information is used locally to match and position notes. The current beta does not send this window information to an advertising or analytics service.

3. Website data

The public website is hosted by Vercel. As part of delivering the site, the hosting provider may process standard request information such as IP address, browser details, requested path, timestamps and security logs. Skribly currently does not add behavioural advertising, tracking pixels or analytics cookies to the website.

4. Downloads

The same-site download endpoint may redirect your browser to the service hosting the current installer. That provider may process normal download request information under its own privacy terms. The website does not require you to visit the source-code repository or create a repository account.

5. Payments

Paid checkout is not active yet. Before payments open, this policy will identify the selected payment provider and explain the billing information that provider processes. Skribly will not store full card, bank or UPI credentials. Payment status and licence entitlement information may be retained when necessary to deliver and support a purchase.

6. Retention and deletion

Deleting a Skrib removes its saved note record. The application also attempts to remove locally stored rich content associated with that note. Uninstalling an application does not always remove user data automatically, so a dedicated export and deletion flow will be documented before stable release.

7. Security and beta limitations

Skribly uses local storage and Windows-native integration, but no beta software can guarantee absolute security. Do not store passwords, payment credentials, private keys or highly sensitive regulated information in the current beta.

8. Children

Skribly is a general productivity utility and is not directed to children under 13. The current website does not knowingly collect account information from children.

9. Changes

This policy may change as licensing, payments, optional diagnostics or cloud features are introduced. Material changes will be reflected by an updated effective date and release notes.

10. Contact

A dedicated support and privacy email will be published before paid checkout opens. Until then, no payment or account data is collected directly by Skribly.
