Refinement decisions

Scheduling should be impossible to misread.

01
One-time only in v0

The Lab no longer demonstrates Daily, Weekdays, or Weekly recurrence ahead of the product contract.

02
Calendar and time share one sheet

No detached popover floats over the writing surface.

03
Notification is a delivery option

The reminder remains useful even when Windows notification permission is unavailable.

Visual hierarchy

Professional refinement without losing the paper metaphor.

Paper is a material, not a theme

Pastels identify Skribs. Management windows stay mostly neutral so the colour remains meaningful.

Three radius levels

8px controls, 12px structured surfaces, 16–20px paper objects. Pills are reserved for counts and compact states.

Elevation follows behaviour

Floating contextual surfaces receive soft depth. Normal windows use borders and low elevation instead of card-on-card shadows.

Typography has jobs

Manrope for strong titles, DM Sans for interface language, Kalam only for authored Skrib content.
