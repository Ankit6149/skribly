Future product system · A-only

Living Paper, built as a product—not a theme.

The note can feel tactile. Photographs can feel like photographs. A reminder can feel like a dated slip. But system behavior stays precise, calm, keyboard-safe and failure-aware. The goal is a tool users stop noticing while they work.

08 · My Skribs

Paper Spine / Ribbon Rail

No enclosing card. A thin edge spine unfolds and individual thoughts slide out as paper strips while the desktop stays visible between them.

YOUR WORK STAYS VISIBLE

Release checklist

The Rail sits on top of the desktop without becoming another application window or blocking the whole side of the screen.

Click the narrow edge seam to unfold the Rail.
Rest 4–7 px

A narrow layered seam on the screen edge. It can auto-hide to zero pixels and always returns through shortcut/tray.

Open 28–40 px spine

The seam widens just enough for Here / Everything / Past / Search. No panel background appears.

Thought strips 34–46 px

Individual Skribs slide inward as separate paper strips. Main strip = Open Here. End-cap ↗ = Return to saved place.

Density bounded

Large result sets scroll through strips; lower-priority results compress into `+N more`. Search handles serious scale.

Primary behavior cannot rely on hover accessibility

Open Here and Return are separately focusable. Hover may add lift/straightening only.

Presence controls required

Always visible / Auto-hide / Hide until shortcut. The user can make Skribli disappear without quitting background behavior.
