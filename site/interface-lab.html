Interaction philosophy

Three levels of pointer response.

01Acknowledge

1–2 px lift, subtle shadow, tiny colour or icon response.

~100–160 ms
02Reveal

Secondary actions or information appear because the user approached.

~160–240 ms
03Transform

~260–420 ms
Design lock

Hover can add meaning and character, but primary actions remain discoverable through visible structure, click/tap, keyboard focus, or explicit lab specimens.
