FUTURE PRODUCT SYSTEM · MAIN APP ONLY

An occasional control room, not another workplace.

The redesigned app is intentionally quieter than the capture experience. It exists to find a thought, understand what is coming back, configure behavior, recover data, manage trust and leave again. The daily product still happens in context—not inside this window.
