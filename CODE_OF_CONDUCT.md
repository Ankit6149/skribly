# Community conduct and maintainer responsibility

Treat participants respectfully. Constructive disagreement about design, implementation and product direction is welcome. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual harassment, doxxing and publication of private user data are unacceptable. Critique the work rather than attacking the person.

Maintainer: Ankit Bhardwaj, GitHub `@Ankit6149`. For a concern, use a private contact route on the maintainer's GitHub profile and share only enough detail to establish a safe follow-up. Do not post sensitive allegations, private evidence or vulnerability details in a public issue. Security reports follow SECURITY.md. A dedicated abuse-reporting address remains a pre-launch community-operations action; do not claim that such a mailbox exists today.

The maintainer will assess reports, protect private information, hear relevant context and apply proportionate action, which may include a warning, removal of content or a participation restriction. Reports involving the maintainer need an independent escalation contact before additional maintainers are onboarded; that appointment is still pending. This document does not promise an unstaffed response SLA.

Keep contributions focused and reviewable. Bulk unsolicited changes, spam and attempts to extract customer data or secrets may be removed. Contributor status does not authorize releases, repository permissions, access to production services or decisions on behalf of the product owner.
