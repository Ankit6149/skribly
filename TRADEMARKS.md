You may accurately identify the upstream software and comply with attribution requirements. Do not represent an independent or modified distribution as an official Skribli release or imply endorsement. Use a distinct product/publisher identity for an independent fork and retain required copyright/licence notices. This policy does not restrict copyright permissions granted under MPL-2.0 or lawful nominative/fair use.

The original app-icon SVG has the software copyright permissions identified in LICENSING.md; that does not grant rights to market a confusingly branded service. Other companies' app/site marks remain subject to their own licences and trademark rights. Permission to render an icon is not permission to claim an affiliation.

Official source builds, private owner packages and accepted public releases are different artifacts. Only releases explicitly identified by the maintainer with their source identity, hashes and release notes should be described as official public packages.
